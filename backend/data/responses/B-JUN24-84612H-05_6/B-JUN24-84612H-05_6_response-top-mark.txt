The contraceptive pill can contain oestrogen and progesterone. These inhibit FSH, so eggs do not mature and there is no mature egg to be fertilised. They also inhibit LH, preventing ovulation, so an egg is not released for fertilisation.

For infertility, FSH can be given to stimulate eggs to mature in the ovaries. LH can then stimulate ovulation, increasing the chance of an egg being fertilised. In IVF, eggs are collected and fertilised outside the body, and an embryo is placed into the uterus. Progesterone can be given to maintain the uterus lining, increasing the chance of the embryo implanting.
