When the animal gets vaccinated its lymphocytes make antibodies that fit the antigens on Salmonella. It also makes memory cells. If it gets infected later the memory cells let it make lots of new antibodies quickly. These stick to the bacteria so white blood cells can destroy them. This means fewer Salmonella bacteria get into things like eggs. People then swallow fewer bacteria when they eat the eggs, so the bacteria are less likely to build up enough to make them ill.
