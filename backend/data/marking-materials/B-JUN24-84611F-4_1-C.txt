Q.04.1 

To gain access to Level 2 (3 marks), students must refer to the reagents needed to test for both types of carbohydrate and must also give one colour (positive or negative) for one of the tests. 
 To achieve the higher mark within this level, the student must address all parts of the question by including the expected positive and negative results for both of the tests as well. 

To gain access to Level 1 (1 mark), students must give an indicative point from the mark scheme. If more than one indicative point is given about one carbohydrate only, then the higher mark may be awarded but the response will remain in Level 1. 
 Note that although heat is not required to achieve Level 2, heat can be used as a creditworthy point in Level 1. For example, ‘Benedict’s tests for sugar and you need to heat it’ would achieve 2 marks in Level 1. Reference to ‘boil’ or ‘heat’ without any context is not creditworthy, whereas ‘boiling or heating Benedict’s (reagent)’ or ‘the sugar test needs heating’ would gain one mark in Level 1.
