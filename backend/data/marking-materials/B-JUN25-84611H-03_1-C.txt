This is an Extended Response question. Please read the information in the introduction to this 
commentary document regarding how to annotate student’s responses. 
You may find it helpful to download the infographic (star-chart) from the additional supporting 
information in OLS. This shows what details are required to gain access (i.e. achieve the lower 
mark) for each Level in a visual format. 
In the mark scheme, there are 5 strengths shown by a black dot and 12 improvements shown 
by a black dot and their corresponding reason(s) shown by a white dot(s). Examiners are 
advised to number the ‘strengths’ bullet points in the mark scheme from 1-5 and the 
improvements and reasons from 1-12. When using the star annotation for strengths, place the 
star on the left-hand side of the student’s response and the improvements and reasons on the 
right-hand side. If the improvement and reason are linked, place the star icons next to each 
other.  
To achieve entry to Level 3, the student must identify at least one strength from the method 
given at the start of the question. There must also be a linked improvement and corresponding 
reason, along with additional improvements / reasons as indicated in the infographic. For entry 
into Level 2, students must identify some improvements or reasons. To achieve entry into Level 
1, a student can give a strength or an improvement or a reason. 
If the student mentions ‘the potato’ this is not sufficient for credit in relation to using the same 
potato (as a strength BP or as an improvement BP). However, if the student states ‘a potato’ or 
‘one potato’ credit should be awarded. Take care not to award credit twice for the same potato 
being used as a strength, and as an improvement.  
For BP5, the idea of ‘repeat’ without qualification is sufficient to gain credit.
