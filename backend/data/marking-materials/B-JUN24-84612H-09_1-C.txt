This is an Extended Response question. Please read the information in the introduction to 
this commentary document regarding how to annotate students' responses. 
You may find it helpful to download the infographic (star-chart) from the additional supporting 
information in OLS. This shows what details are required to gain access (i.e. achieve the 
lower mark) for each Level in a visual format. 
Most of the points in the mark scheme are structured in terms of linked ideas. These are 
represented by ‘black bullet points’ and ‘white bullet points’ So to get credit for linked ideas, 
as described in the Generic Level Descriptors at the top of the mark scheme, students must 
give a black bullet point along with one of its associated white bullet points. 
Beware of bp2 in the indicative content. Credit should only be given if the student refers to 
reduced competition for some factor specified, not just ‘resources’ or ‘nutrients’ which are too 
vague. Furthermore, many students having referred to bp1, then state words such as ‘…so 
the GM soya gets more light’ – this is also not sufficient for bp2 as it does not refer to 
reduced competition, well enough, at this level of demand. 
Access to Level 2 can be achieved by describing a linked idea. Thus, ‘more light for 
photosynthesis so more glucose is produced’ would achieve this, as would ‘more 
magnesium to make chlorophyll’. You will see that there are many ways that this level can 
be reached, as any black bullet point along with an associated white bullet point is all that is 
required. 
To reach Level 3, two linked ideas are required, along with a clear indication that glyphosate 
kills weeds but does not affect GM soya (the first black bullet point). 
The details above describe what is required to reach the lower mark (1, 3 or 5) in each of the 
three Levels. In each case, just one additional piece of information is required to achieve the 
higher mark in the respective level. However, several unlinked ideas might be given but this 
will not raise the mark beyond the higher mark in that Level.
