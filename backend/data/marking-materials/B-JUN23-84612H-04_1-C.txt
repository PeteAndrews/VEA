This is an ‘Extended Response’ style of question. Such questions are marked holistically. You will see that there are overall generic descriptions for the two levels of response at the top of the scheme, giving a hierarchy of response. Within each Level of Response there are three marks. 

The indicative content has been divided into four blocks of bullet points. The first block refers to how microorganisms break down the plant material. The second block refers to what is produced in this process. The third block refers to the use of the carbon dioxide produced, and the fourth block refers to the role of nitrates in the recycling process. The production of chlorophyll would be a suitable other example of the use of nitrate ions; if the student gives any other examples, please check online. 

To gain access to Level 2 a student must give details of the key ideas of the role of microorganisms in the production of CO2 and the use of nitrates and CO2 by the plant. 

To gain access to Level 1 a student must refer to one key idea with one other idea from any of the four blocks. Answers which then add additional indicative content allow the students to move up through Level 1 and Level 2 providing they have met the benchmark required for entry into the Level. 

It will help your judgement of the level if you put stars in the left margin for the key ideas and in the right margin for other points. 

As a reminder, the list of indicative content is not exhaustive, so credit can be given for other ideas that are of the same standard.
