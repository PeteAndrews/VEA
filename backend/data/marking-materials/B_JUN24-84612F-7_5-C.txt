In this extended response question it is important to remember that what is printed on the mark scheme is Indicative Content i.e. there may be other responses which are worthy of credit. 

Level 1 answers will contain points which are not linked. 
Level 2 answers will contain points which are clearly and correctly linked. Words such as because, so, which, as, whereas are good indicators that two points may be linked. 

Remember that valid links are not limited to adjacent solid and open bullet points in the mark scheme. Read the whole answer and credit sound statements. Think about the meaning of what the student has written and do not expect to see phrases in the answer which are identical to those in the mark scheme. Eg bp2 there is no variation = identical. You may see reverse arguments which should be credited if correct. 
 Be careful that you do not give two marks for the same marking point which has been given in a slightly different way twice in the answer. If a student uses the term “genetic information” it may be given credit but only to raise them within Level 1 i.e. from 1 mark to 2 marks. It is too vague above this level.
