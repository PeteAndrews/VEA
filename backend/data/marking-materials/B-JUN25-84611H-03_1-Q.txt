A student investigated osmosis in pieces of potato. 
  
This is the method used. 
 
1. Cut a piece of potato into a cube. 
 
2. Record the mass of the cube. 
 
3. Place the cube into a beaker containing 0.4 mol/dm3 salt solution. 
 
4. After some time, remove the cube and record the mass. 
 
5. Calculate the change in the mass of the cube. 
 
6. Repeat steps 1 to 5 using two more different concentrations of salt solution.

Q03.1 
 
Evaluate the student’s method. 
 
You should include how the student could improve the validity of their results.
