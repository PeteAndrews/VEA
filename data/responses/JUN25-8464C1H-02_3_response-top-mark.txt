Measure 50 cm³ of water using a measuring cylinder and pour it into a polystyrene cup. Use a thermometer to record the starting temperature. Weigh 1 g of calcium oxide on a balance, add it to the water and stir. Record the highest temperature reached and subtract the starting temperature from this to calculate the temperature rise.

Repeat using fresh water with 2 g, 3 g, 4 g and 5 g of calcium oxide. Use 50 cm³ of water each time and make sure it starts at the same temperature. Use the same cup and repeat each mass three times to calculate a mean temperature rise for each mass.
